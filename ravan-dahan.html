        How to play

        Press anywhere on the ground and drag away from where you want the arrow to go, like pulling a bowstring. Let go to shoot. Watch the wind at the top left. Once all ten heads are down, the navel glows. Hit it to light the fire.

        The ten heads

        Each head stands for a vice from the tradition: Kaam, Krodh, Lobh, Moh, Mada, Matsarya, Swartha, Anyaya, Amanavta and Ahankar. Ravan only fell when Ram's arrow, on Vibhishan's advice, reached the amrit in his navel.

        Run it at the office

        1. The host presses "Start live show" and shares this screen with audio.
        2. Everyone opens the link on their phone, picks Team Ram or Team Hanuman, and taps to fill their bar.
        3. The host asks Ramayana questions; right answers add bonus Shakti.
        4. The team that strikes the navel burns Ravan.
